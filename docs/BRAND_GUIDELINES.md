# Project Status

KWC Connect is an independent software project developed by Fathima Shajahan and Aniga.

The application is intended to be presented to the Kerala Women's Commission. The branding defined in this document represents the proposed visual identity of the application. It does not imply official approval, endorsement, ownership, or adoption by the Kerala Women's Commission or the Government of Kerala.

---

# 1. Brand Overview

KWC Connect is a proposed digital platform designed for the Kerala Women's Commission.

The project aims to modernize women's support services through a secure, accessible, and user-centered digital experience. It is currently under development by Fathima Shajahan and Aniga for future presentation to the Kerala Women's Commission.

---

# 2. Government Identity Hierarchy

KWC Connect is designed to align with the professionalism, accessibility, and public service principles of the Kerala Women's Commission. If adopted in the future, the application should represent the authority and credibility of the Commission while remaining approachable and user-friendly.

The proposed branding hierarchy is as follows:

Government of Kerala
↓
Kerala Women's Commission
↓
KWC Connect

**Intended Application of Hierarchy:**
- **Splash Screen:** Visual alignment with the Government of Kerala identity followed by the KWC logo.
- **Mobile & Web:** Headers reflecting the Commission's identity.
- **Documents:** Proposed layouts for PDFs and receipts following official Commission standards.

---

# 3. Brand Mission

To provide a secure, accessible, and dignified digital gateway that enables the women of Kerala to access justice, protection, and support services. The project aims to bridge the gap between citizens and the Commission through technology that prioritizes user safety and emotional well-being.

---

# 4. Brand Vision

To become a trusted and inclusive digital service platform that sets a high standard for how technology can be used to protect and empower citizens within a government framework.

---

---

# 6. Brand Personality & Principles

The application is designed to feel like a **"Compassionate Guardian."**

- **Calm:** Interfaces use a limited color palette to avoid visual noise.
- **Professional:** Precise alignment and margins following an 8dp grid system.
- **Government-grade:** High standards of reliability, security, and authority.
- **Inclusive:** Visuals representing women from all backgrounds in Kerala.
- **Respectful:** Focusing on resilience rather than victimhood.
- **Human-centered:** Designing specifically for users in potentially high-stress situations.

---

# 7. Design Principles

- **Simplicity over decoration:** Every visual element must have a functional purpose.
- **Readability over complexity:** Content must be legible at a glance.
- **Accessibility before aesthetics:** Compliance with accessibility standards is a priority.
- **Credibility over trends:** Avoiding "startup" aesthetics in favor of a timeless, stable look.
- **Consistency across platforms:** Seamless experience between mobile and web.

---

# 8. Logo Meaning

The proposed KWC Connect logo is a visual metaphor for the project's goals:
- **The Silhouette:** Represents the modern Kerala woman—strong and resilient.
- **The Raised Arm:** Signifies a call for justice and reaching for support.
- **The Mountain/Triangle:** Represents the stable foundation and protection offered by the Commission.
- **Stree Shakti (स्त्री शक्ति):** Reinforces the mission of "Women's Power."

---

# 9. Logo Usage Guidelines

| Variant | Usage |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Logo** | Used for Splash Screens and Login pages. |
| **Horizontal Logo** | Used for Web Headers and Mobile Navigation bars. |
| **Vertical Logo** | Used for marketing materials and centered landing page sections. |
| **Icon Only** | App Icons, Favicons, and Profile placeholders. |
| **White Version** | Used on Deep Maroon or Deep Navy backgrounds. |
| **Dark Version** | Standard for Light Theme usage. |
| **Monochrome** | Used for official stencils and notifications. |

---

# 10. Logo Protection Rules

To maintain professional integrity, **NEVER**:
- Rotate or flip the logo.
- Stretch or distort proportions.
- Crop the silhouette or text elements.
- Add shadows, glows, or gradients.
- Change official colors.
- Place on low-contrast backgrounds.

---

# 11. Color Philosophy

The palette is derived from the official Design System:
- **Deep Maroon (#7A1F3D):** Strength, courage, and primary identity.
- **Deep Navy (#12355B):** Law, authority, and stability.
- **Warm Gold (#C89B3C):** Recognition, hope, and excellence.
- **Soft Warm White (#FFF6F7):** A welcoming, safe, and non-clinical background.

---

# 12. Light Theme Identity

The Light Theme represents a **"Safe Space."** It uses the Soft Warm White background to create a feeling of comfort. Typography is primarily Deep Navy for maximum legibility, and generous spacing is used to reduce user stress during interactions.

---

# 13. Dark Theme Identity

The Dark Theme represents a **"Professional Sanctuary."** It **NEVER uses OLED Pure Black** to avoid eye strain during long-form reading. It utilizes Deep Navy-Grey tones (#1B2430) to maintain a professional, government-grade dashboard aesthetic.

---

# 14. Typography Philosophy

- **Authority (Serif):** Lora/Playfair Display for Headings to communicate weight and institutional heritage.
- **Utility (Sans-Serif):** Inter/Poppins for Body text to ensure readability on mobile screens.
- **Multilingual Support:** Full optimization for both English and Malayalam scripts.

---

# 15. Illustration Style

- **Prefer:** Empowerment, community, confidence, and diversity.
- **Avoid:** Violence, fear, victim imagery, or graphic abuse.
- **Style:** Flat, minimalist illustrations representing a wide range of citizens.

---

# 16. Iconography

- **Stroke Width:** Consistent 2px weight.
- **Style:** Rounded corners to appear supportive and approachable.
- **Fill vs Outline:** Outline for default states; Filled for active states.
- **Accessibility:** Clear metaphors that are easily understood by all users.

---

# 17. Motion Principles

Animations must be **calm and predictable**:
- **Splash:** A gentle fade-in of the logo.
- **Navigation:** Standard horizontal slides.
- **Buttons:** Subtle color shifts; no elastic or "bouncing" motions.
- **Loading:** Simple, rhythmic Maroon indicators.

---

# 18. Content Tone

The application uses **Professional, Respectful, and Clear** language.

| **Avoid (Startup Tone)** | **Use (Professional Tone)** |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Awesome!" / "Great Job!" | "Complaint Submitted Successfully" |
| "You're all set!" | "Verification Pending" |
| "Oops! Something went wrong." | "An error occurred. Please try again." |

---

# 19. Accessibility

- **WCAG AA:** Minimum compliance for all interfaces.
- **Screen Readers:** Mandatory descriptive labels for all elements.
- **Touch Targets:** Minimum 48x48dp.
- **Multilingual:** High readability in both English and Malayalam.
- **Color Blindness:** Information is never conveyed by color alone.

# 22. Brand Governance Checklist

Before any screen is implemented, it must satisfy:
- [ ] **Logo:** Uses the correct variant for the context.
- [ ] **Colors:** Adheres strictly to the official palette tokens.
- [ ] **Typography:** Maintains the defined Serif/Sans-Serif hierarchy.
- [ ] **Accessibility:** Passes WCAG AA contrast and touch target tests.
- [ ] **Responsive:** Works seamlessly on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- [ ] **Tone:** Uses professional and respectful language.
- [ ] **Dark Mode:** Fully compatible and avoids OLED pure black.
- [ ] **Multilingual:** Fully functional in English and Malayalam.
- [ ] **Design Tokens:** Uses centralized tokens instead of hard-coded values.
